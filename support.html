薄肌 帮助

「掉的是脂肪还是肌肉」怎么看？

用能测体脂率的体重秤称重并同步到「健康」。有两次以上、隔一周以上的称重，首屏就会把体重变化拆成脂肪和去脂体重。体脂秤单次读数误差大，薄肌比较的是最早 3 次和最近 3 次的平均，看几周的趋势。

怎么拍食物？

打开「扫饭菜」，对着食物慢慢移动手机，不用按快门。每样食物上方会浮出千焦和蛋白质；Pro 机型绕着食物转一圈，重量量得更准。

认错了怎么办？

点那个数字，选「自己选」改成正确的食物。薄肌会记住，下次自动认出。没被认出来的东西，直接在画面上点它一下。

包装食品怎么算？

把包装转一圈，让手机看到净含量和背面的营养成分表，或者条码。剩一半的话，在卡片上选「一半」。

数字准吗？

包装食品按营养表计算，比较准；现做的饭菜是估算，误差可能有两三成。所有数值和建议仅供参考，不替代医生或营养师。

数据安全吗？

所有分析都在手机上完成，不上传照片和健康数据。详见 隐私政策。

Boji Support

How does "fat or muscle?" work?

Weigh yourself on a scale that measures body fat and syncs to Apple Health. With two or more weigh-ins at least a week apart, the first screen splits your weight change into fat and lean mass. Single readings are noisy, so Boji compares the average of the first 3 and the latest 3 and looks at weeks, not days.

How do I scan food?

Open Scan and move your phone slowly over your food. No shutter button needed. Each food gets a floating label with kilojoules and protein; on Pro iPhones, circle the food for a better weight.

It got a food wrong

Tap the number, choose the right food, and Boji remembers it next time. If something isn't picked up at all, tap it on screen.

Packaged food

Turn the package so the phone can see the net contents and the nutrition panel, or the barcode. If it's half finished, choose "Half" on the card.

How accurate is it?

Packaged food uses the label, so it's close. Freshly cooked meals are estimates and can be 20 to 30% off. Numbers and advice are general information, not medical advice.
