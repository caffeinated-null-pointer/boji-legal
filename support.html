薄肌 帮助

怎么拍食物？

打开「拍食物」，对着食物慢慢移动手机，不用按快门。每样食物上方会浮出千焦数字；Pro 机型绕着食物转一圈，重量量得更准。

认错了怎么办？

点那个数字，选「自己选」改成正确的食物。薄肌会记住，下次自动认出。没被认出来的东西，直接在画面上点它一下。

包装食品怎么算？

把包装转一圈，让手机看到净含量和背面的营养成分表，或者条码。剩一半的话，在卡片上选「一半」。

数字准吗？

包装食品按营养表计算，比较准；现做的饭菜是估算，误差可能有两三成。所有数值和建议仅供参考，不替代医生或营养师。

数据安全吗？

所有分析都在手机上完成，不上传照片和健康数据。详见 隐私政策。

Boji Support

How do I scan food?

Open Scan and move your phone slowly over your food. No shutter button needed. Each food gets a floating kilojoule label; on Pro iPhones, circle the food for a better weight.

It got a food wrong

Tap the number, choose the right food, and Boji remembers it next time. If something isn't picked up at all, tap it on screen.

Packaged food

Turn the package so the phone can see the net contents and the nutrition panel, or the barcode. If it's half finished, choose "Half" on the card.

How accurate is it?

Packaged food uses the label, so it's close. Freshly cooked meals are estimates and can be 20 to 30% off. Numbers and advice are general information, not medical advice.

Privacy

Everything is analysed on your phone; no photos or health data are uploaded. See the privacy policy.
